Формула пасивного стану: Subject + to be (у потрібному часі) + Past Participle (V3 / -ed)
Виконавець вказується прийменником by, інструмент чи матеріал — with.
Tense (Час) Структура Passive Практичний приклад (Active → Passive)
Present Simpleam / is / are + V3We maintain fair prices. → Fair prices are maintained.
Present Continuousam / is / are + being + V3They are reviewing the application. → The application is being reviewed.
Past Simplewas / were + V3The manager signed the agreement. → The agreement was signed.
Past Continuouswas / were + being + V3They were discussing the agenda. → The agenda was being discussed.
Present Perfecthave / has + been + V3We have captured market share. → Market share has been captured.
Past Perfecthad + been + V3The firm had reached the settlement. → The settlement had been reached.
Future Simplewill be + V3The board will uphold the standards. → Standards will be upheld.
Future Perfectwill have been + V3By May they will have built it. → It will have been built by May.
Modal Verbsmodal + be + V3You must obey the law. → The law must be obeyed.
Modal Perfectmodal + have been + V3He should have informed us. → We should have been informed.
Важливі винятки та особливості B2:
  • Perfect Continuous (Present/Past/Future) не вживаються у пасивному стані — замість них використовують Simple Perfect (напр., has been investigated for three weeks).
  • Дієслова з двома додатками (give, send, offer, tell): підметом стає особа: The best participant was offered a managerial position.
  • Безособовий пасив (Impersonal Passive): в офіційних звітах: It is acknowledged that sustainability is vital.
Правило узгодження часів (Backshift): якщо головне речення стоїть у минулому часі (said, stated, told, asked, noted, promised, warned), дієслово прямої мови робить «крок назад» у часі. У спеціальних питаннях зберігається прямий порядок слів!
